# Landing hero

The home page (`/`): the pixel globe with a typographic hero over it. Design source: the owner's canvas boards "home-rest" and "home-touched" (2026-10-12; 1440 x 900, dark scheme; both schemes are built from the app tokens, nothing is copied from the boards' hex values). All type, no boxes, no ornament; the accent appears once (a row or header item under the pointer or the keyboard). Identity change (2026-10-13): the big type is the owner's NAME, not the project's code name, and the four rows replace the header's section links on this page.

Round 3 (2026-10-13, owner feedback items 3 and 10; reworked 2026-10-14 after the owner's review): the home page opens fully zoomed OUT and, at rest, shows the planet with the flat **ring of type** as it always was (zoomed out) and a **caption** wrapped on a sphere (round 3 drew it in the globe's own pixel pass; since round 4 it is crisp, in a second canvas), instead of boxes and labels; the caption replaces the how-to line; the planet is **lifted** clear of the name; and the collapse **shrinks the name** into the corner, the name itself being what stays there. See "Showcase" below.

Code: `app/components/landing-hero.tsx` (the markup and the name's travel), `app/lib/hero-machine.ts` (the state machine and `showcaseOf`, pure), `app/hooks/use-landing-hero.ts` (wiring to the shell: the machine, the showcase signal and the measured cover), `app/routes/shell.tsx` (mounts it), the "Landing hero" block at the end of `app/app.css`. Check: `scripts/home/hero.mjs`; the globe's side: `scripts/globe/ring.mjs`.

## Structure

```

  h1.hero-name        "Elliot" (.hero-given, 800) + "Négrel-Jerzy" (.hero-family .type-thin, 200), Bricolage, .type-display-xl; the page's h1
  .hero-lead
    p.hero-intro      who and what: "Front-end and UX software engineer based in Warsaw." + span.hero-intro-more " Data visualisation, geospatial and cartography interfaces, design systems, computer graphics." (the second sentence goes in a very short window)
    p.hero-hint       how: "Drag the globe to explore and pick a place, or start from a list." (muted, a step smaller; gone in a short window; without scripts: "Start from a list."). While the globe has the caption (`data-orbit` "pending" or "true") it is visually hidden and stays in the accessibility tree: the caption is the cue, this is the text.
    button.hero-search  [data-needs-script] the search icon (control size), "Search places and entries" (mono 13), the key hint (command icon + K, or Ctrl K); opens the existing palette
  nav.hero-list       aria-label "Primary" (the home page's navigation; the header has none here)
    ul > li > a.hero-row  Software, Articles, Artworks, Poetry and, only when the projection has a career document, Career (a fifth row, `data-rows="5"`; not a content kind, so not in the caption): hairline rows, Bricolage 30 / 300, an arrow icon at 1.1 em (33 px)
  p.hero-mini         aria-hidden: the GHOST of the collapsed name (span.hero-mini-given + span.hero-mini-family, set like the big name, never painted: the boxes the big name's two halves travel to and stay on) and the hint "Drag to explore. Back to start after a moment."
```

Layout (wide): intro, hint and search left (470 px), the sections' rows right (360 px; five sections and the career row, Photography included), the name under them: ONE line from 48 rem, **deliberately not the full width** (owner, round 2: "clearly narrower"): it takes `--hero-span` 0.72 of the viewport (`--hero-size` = 100vw x 0.72 / `--hero-fit` 6.6 em, the name's measured width at 800 / 200: 157 px at 1440, which is 1037 px, 72 %; 737 px at 1024; 565 px, 74 %, at 768 where the optical size is a little wider), never over a third of the height nor 30 rem. Weights: the given name is the heavy half (800, set at -0.02 em: at the name's -0.055 em the three l, l, i of "Elliot" touched), the family name the thin half (200, -0.055 em). The hyphen of the family name has its own span (`.hero-hyphen`, 0.045 em of margin). Gutters 56 px (`clamp(1rem, 3.9vw, 3.5rem)`). Under 48 rem it stacks in two lines: the given name over the family name, whose width is what fills the row there (`--hero-fit` 4.7, gutter to gutter: 76 px at 390), then intro, hint, search, rows. Wide and short (under 34 rem tall, from 48 rem wide): the rows in two columns, the name capped at 26 vh and the hint dropped (the globe is in plain view; the line saved is room for it).

**The whole block is always in the window** (review of 2026-10-13: at 667 x 375, 720 x 450 = a 1440 x 900 window at 200 % zoom, 320 x 568 and 320 x 480 the stacked block was 510 to 540 px tall, bottom-anchored in an `overflow: hidden` main, so the name was at negative y). From 320 x 480 and from 568 x 320 up:
- The stacked name's size is also what the height leaves (`--hero-size` takes `(100dvh - --hero-fixed - --hero-clear - safe area - 1rem) / 1.9`; `--hero-fixed` is what the block holds besides the name, per tier: 26.5 rem, 24.5 rem without the hint, 17 rem with the rows in two columns, 13.5 rem without the second sentence), never over the width fit and 24 vh, never under 1.75 rem.
- Tiers under 48 rem: up to 40 rem tall the how-to line is gone; up to 36 rem the four rows are two columns of 44 px targets; under 30 rem wide and up to 28 rem tall only who the owner is stays of the intro (the second sentence is its own span, `.hero-intro-more`).
- A phone on its side, 30 to 48 rem wide and up to 34 rem tall: the wide layout in small (intro and search left, the two-by-two rows right, the name on ONE line across the bottom at 72 % of the width, 26 vh at most); at 22 rem tall, or 28 rem under 40 rem wide, also without the second sentence.
- The dev badge raises the block by 4.75 rem under 48 rem (`--hero-clear`): the tiers hold with it. Below 320 x 480 (320 x 320 and the like, which no phone has) the block can still overflow by up to 25 px: not supported.
- The sizes are measured in `scripts/home/hero.mjs` (15 shapes, light and dark, top and bottom of the block's box against the window, the globe reachable above it). DOM order is reading order (name, intro, search, rows); the grid places the name last on a wide screen.

The block is bottom-anchored and rises by the credits line's height plus what the "y" hangs under the line box (`padding-bottom: clear + 0.15 * name size`), so no letter is ever over the credits line (bottom right, 8 to 30 px from the bottom) or, in development, the content badge. The hero is `pointer-events: none`; only the search line and the rows take the pointer, so the globe stays draggable and zoomable beneath all the rest. A page-colour fade (`.landing-hero::before`, no blur) sits behind the thin type.

## Showcase (round 3)

While the hero is `rest` at the world view (`showcaseOf(snapshot, cover)`: `state === "rest" && world`) the home screen is a **showcase**: nothing else of the globe's overlay competes with the caption.

- **Start zoomed out.** The page opens at the globe's lowest zoom (`GlobeRenderer.startView`: `minZoom`, the whole-globe fit minus the room the ring needs, 0.5 levels at every checked viewport) instead of the fit; the flat ring of type (unchanged, text from `SITE`) is shown from the first frame. `GlobeViewState.zoom` still reports 0 below the fit. A debug page (`?globe-debug`) keeps the old start unless `?showcase`.
- **What changes.** The boxes and labels fade out (`LodTree.setSuppressed`: every node's target goes OFF and runs by its own timed opacity, `FADE_MS` 200 ms, no re-planning) while the caption's lines come round the planet from behind (`docs/web-architecture.md` "Caption on the sphere": 900 ms ease-out-expo, each row 450 ms after the one before, each from a random side; on load and every time the showcase returns). The hidden boxes are inert (a node that is off takes no pick, and `pickAt` answers null while the showcase is on). The first gesture (a press or a wheel over the globe: the engine's own capture listener, and the hero's `onInteract`) ends it at once: the boxes come back, the lines go on behind the planet in 200 ms and the press that began it selects nothing. The places list, the keyboard and the URL paths are untouched (a place opening takes the hero to `collapsed` and ends it).
- **The planet is lifted.** While the showcase is on the planet's centre goes UP by what makes its lowest point clear the name (the hero measures `name.offsetTop` from the page's bottom: `LandingHero` `onCover`, a `ResizeObserver`, layout offsets so the name's own transform never changes it) plus 14 px, as far as the room above allows (`liftFor`, `engine/lift.ts`: the highest thing, the planet or the ring's top, keeps 14 px from the top edge). It follows the cover when it changes, tweened like the panel's inset (`TUNING.insetMs`, `INSET_EASE`), and comes down in the same time when the showcase ends. Reduced motion: at once. The lowest zoom, the fit and the framing clamps use the same centre (`liftBuf` in every projection: the camera's view offset, the markers, the label tree's `centreY`, the sky's disc, the ring's centre).
- **Words.** The ring is the identity line unchanged (`ringPhrases(SITE)`). The caption is `siteCaption(counts)` in `lib/site.ts`, English, up to five rows in view: the call to action "Drag to explore." / "Pick a place." stays at the bottom (the same size as every row) and the rows above it (two, or three for the section names and their counts: two sections to a row, "Photography" alone on the third) take turns every 5.5 s: "A personal archive," / "organised around places." (all of them drawn in capitals, the page's text is as written; the rows are on the equator, so the camera is held inside a latitude band while the caption shows: a return after a tilt first glides the latitude, then the entrance); after it comes in each row ROLLS slowly through the centre (8 degrees either side over the 5.5 s dwell, in the direction it came in from), then accelerates out and the next phrase set comes in the same way; the sections' own public names from `SECTIONS`, two to a row with their dots ("Software" in the code face, "Poetry" in a calligraphic script); the counts from the projection ("33 software . 12 articles" / "9 artworks . 5 poems", left out when unknown). Interface copy and facts, no code name can reach it. Set in Newsreader (the intro's face) with a few heavier words: text-coloured letters (the secondary text tone; two words at most in BSoD Blue) in a page-coloured contour and thickness that cut the globe's lines round each glyph. The sets stop turning while the user interacts, while the tab is hidden, when the hero is collapsed or away, and under reduced motion (set 1, static); the page's hidden how-to line and the rows are the complete static copy.
- **Leaving.** A gesture ends the showcase: the caption FADES (180 ms, only its opacity) and stays locked to the globe while it does (zoom, pinch, drag); the boxes and labels come back and the flat ring leaves with them, in the same frames and on the same clock (it is back with the caption when the hero returns to rest). Closing a panel or collapsing the hero never flashes either.
- **Accessibility.** The caption is pixels in the globe's canvas (not in the accessibility tree, not selectable). The how-to line is visually hidden (not removed) while the caption is there; without scripts (`scripting: none`), without WebGL, with `?no-ring` and on the empty archive (`data-orbit="false"`) it shows as before.
- **Off switches.** `?no-ring` (the ring AND the caption, the lowest zoom below the fit, the showcase): the page is the previous one, the boxes stay and the hint shows.

## Room for the globe (phones and tablets, 2026-10-20)

The caption (the 3D words) must never run over a line of the hero, and the globe should be as big as that allows. The hero reports the blocks of the home screen over the globe (`GlobeShowcase.blocks`: the name, the intro and search, the list; their span across the container and the y of their top, measured by `LandingHero`; the name from layout offsets, so its own travel never moves it). The globe works out its size and place itself (`engine/lift.ts` `placeAbove`, used by `GlobeRenderer.lowestFor`):

- **The caption's lowest line sits `LIFT.gapPx` above the highest block under the column the words span** (`CAPTION_HALF_R`, 1.04 radii to each side of the centre; the lowest line is `CAPTION_BOTTOM_R`, 0.32 radii below the centre: measured on the page). The planet's own lower part may go under the content: it is a drawing that fades into the page behind the hero's scrim; only the words must be readable.
- **That is the minimum; more room is used.** The planet may be as big as its whole ring fits the window (`ringRoomRadius`, no longer capped half a level under the whole-globe fit), and between the highest and the lowest place it can take, the globe sits in the MIDDLE of the free room: the space above its ring equal to the space between its bottom and what stands under it (the whole planet clear of the content when there is room for that).
- **The biggest planet that does it**: searched from the largest the window allows downward, since a smaller planet has a narrower column and can clear a block a larger one would stand over (a wide window keeps its big planet between the text at its sides; on a tablet the list rises into the column). The ring's top stays `LIFT.marginPx` inside the window, and the centre is as low as the middle of the page allows (never lower: the globe is not pushed down).
- **The lowest zoom is that size** (`minZoom`), the lift is the centre's rise, and a view resting at the old floor follows it when the blocks change.
- **The ring (flat type round the planet) is cut where the content begins** (`RingLayer.setCut`: a mask from the highest block's top under its column), so no ring letter runs over the text; the planet and the caption are not cut.
- **No letters that would be cut**: when the planet at that zoom is under 60 px of radius (`MIN_TEXT_PLANET_PX`), neither ring nor caption is drawn (their letters have a smallest size); the planet is still there.
- A phone upright holds the rows in two columns (five rows are three lines), and a short one drops the second sentence of the intro, so the globe has more of the page.
- A page that gives no blocks (only `coverBottom`) keeps the earlier lift (`liftFor`: the planet and its ring clear of one cover height).

Checked in the browser pane at 375 x 812, 320 x 568, 812 x 375, 850 x 1043, 768 x 1024 and 2000 x 1158. Not checked: a real phone, Safari, a tablet on its side.

## States

| State | When | What shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `rest` | load; or idle at the world view after a gesture | the whole hero |
| `collapsed` | after a gesture on the globe; or whenever the view is not the world view (zoomed in, a place open) | the small line bottom-left (left 40 px, bottom 32 px: the name in Bricolage 30: "Elliot" at 800 and "Négrel-Jerzy" at 200, as in the hero, and the hint). The h1 stays in the accessibility tree at opacity 0; the search line and the rows are `visibility: hidden` (not tab stops). The hint is shown only while the way back is real (`data-world="true"`) |
| `away` | a place or an entry is open | nothing is shown or exposed (instant); the name renders as a `p`, so the panel's title is the only h1 |

**Closing a panel never flashes the hero (2026-10-14 fix).** `away` hides only the hero's container (`visibility: hidden`, no transition), so every part inside must already wear the COLLAPSED look while away: the intro, the how-to line, the search line, the rows and the plate's `::before` are keyed on `[data-hero="collapsed"], [data-hero="away"]` in app.css. Before, they kept their resting styles under `away` and the way back to `collapsed` faded them out in the open (the intro appeared and faded). The machine's side is a test: from `away` the state goes straight to `collapsed` when the globe is zoomed in on the place, and to `rest` only at the world view after the normal idle delay (`hero-machine.test.ts`, "a place or an entry opens and closes"); `landing-hero.test.ts` holds the selectors; `hero.mjs` samples the intro, hint, rows and search line every frame for a second after Escape from a place and fails above 5 % opacity (it read 1.0 before the fix).

Transitions: opacity and transform only (visibility follows the fade), `--duration-base` (220 ms) with `--ease-standard`; `prefers-reduced-motion`: none (instant). No JS animation loop; a state change costs one React re-render of the hero, and only on a gesture start, the world view being left or reached, or the idle timer.

**The name's travel (owner, items 10 and 3).** On `rest` -> `collapsed` the big name's two halves (`.hero-given`, `.hero-family`, `inline-block`, `transform-origin: 0 0`) shrink and travel to the corner (the ghost line's boxes, `.hero-mini-given` and `.hero-mini-family`), and `collapsed` -> `rest` is the same motion reversed. The halves ARE the collapsed name: there is no second element to hand over to. A FLIP done once at the state change (`travelName` in `landing-hero.tsx`, in a layout effect, before paint): the two text boxes of each half are measured (`Range.getBoundingClientRect`), the scale is the ratio of their widths (ONE uniform scale per half: the ghost is the same drawing at another size, its optical size pinned at the big name's) and the translation puts the box's corner on the other's; the final transform is set as the half's own style at the start (a travel asked for while one is running, collapse then restore within the 320 ms, starts from where the half is: `lib/name-travel.ts`, tested as behaviour) and `Element.animate` runs two `transform` keyframes from identity to it at `NAME_TRAVEL_MS` 320 ms (`--hero-travel`) with `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.9, 0.1, 1)` (a fast start, a soft landing) on the compositor: no font size, no layout, no per-frame JS, no `will-change` outside the animation, and when it ends the animation is cancelled and the same inline transform is the same picture (no frame between). Back: the inline transform is removed at the start and the animation runs from it. While it is held, a resize or a font arriving re-measures it (`ResizeObserver`). The ghost line is `--hero-mini-size` = `clamp(1.75rem, 9.5vw, 2.75rem)`: 44 px at 1440 px, 37 px at 390 px, 30 px at 320 px (it was 30 px, a scale of 0.2; it is 0.28 now), so the corner name clears the credits line, the dev badge and the hint beside it. The rest of the block (intro, hint) fades and slides as before; the hint of the corner line fades in over the last 90 ms. Reduced motion: no animation (the CSS is instant, as before) and the name is held at once. The h1 stays in the accessibility tree and on screen (it is the visible name now), the focus hold, the tab order and the server markup are unchanged; `hero.mjs` checks the hydration warnings.

The machine (`createHeroMachine`): `rest` goes to `collapsed` on `interact()` or when `setWorld(false)`; `collapsed` goes to `rest` after `HERO_IDLE_MS` (8000, the same delay as the globe's idle rotation) without an input, only while the world view holds. Every input restarts the clock; leaving the world view stops it and coming back restarts it. At most one timer, none at rest. **The clock is held while the keyboard focus is in the header (`[data-slot="site-header"]`) or in an open dialog (the search palette)**: the way back removes the header (the rows are the sections then), so a hero that came back under a focused header link, or under the palette whose opener is the header's trigger, left the focus on `body`. `HeroMachine.hold(held)`, fed by `watchFocusHold` (`hooks/use-landing-hero.ts`: `focusin` and `focusout` on the document); releasing starts a whole idle period. "World view": `GlobeViewState.zoom <= 0.03` and no street scale (`isWorldView`; the idle rotation's own slack, 0.15 zoom levels of a range of about 5.3), with no place or entry open.

## Hooks into the globe

- **The showcase** (round 3): `GlobeProps.showcase`, a signal (`{ get(), subscribe() }`, `useLandingHero().showcase`) over the machine and the measured cover: `{ on, coverBottom }`. The globe subscribes, so a gesture or the idle period moves the globe's state without the shell or the globe's props rendering. `GlobeProps.captionPhrases` are the caption's words, `GlobeProps.onOrbit(available)` tells the hero whether the caption exists (it hides its own how-to line only then). Engine side: `GlobeRenderer.setShowcase`, `setCover`, `LodTree.setSuppressed`.
- **A gesture**: `GlobeProps.onInteract` (`globe/types.ts`), implemented in `globe/globe-canvas.tsx`: two passive capture listeners (`pointerdown`, `wheel`) on the globe's own stage, which holds the canvas, so pointer presses (drag, tap on a place, a pinch's fingers) and wheel (including the trackpad pinch) count, hovering does not, the app's overlays and the credits control are outside it, the globe's own motion (rotation, flights, inertia) is not input. The globe has no keyboard zoom (the canvas is not focusable, the places list is the keyboard path), so there is none to hook. No engine file changed.
- **The view**: the shell already receives every reported view (`onViewChange`); `useLandingHero().onView` compares it with the world view and tells the machine only when that changes.
- **The search**: `openSearch()` (`components/search-palette.tsx`) dispatches a window event the palette (mounted once by the navigation) listens to. The hero's line is a `button` (`aria-haspopup="dialog"`, `aria-keyshortcuts`), not a second search; Cmd/Ctrl-K is unchanged.

## The moon (#312)

A small pixel-art moon circles the planet at the world view and is a link to the first version of the site, which stays as it was: a way back to look at, not a warning. Hover or focus it and it holds still and a card says what it is ("Memory lane", "The first version of this site, kept just as it was.", "Take a trip back ↗"); a click opens `ARCHIVED_V1.url` in a new tab. The address and the words are `ARCHIVED_V1` in `lib/site.ts` (today the old app's own project, `https://v1.bsodium.fr`, see owner-guide.md): the one place to change if the archive moves. The search palette has a "Memory lane" entry (found by "moon", "v1", "version", "old", "first", "archive") for anywhere the moon is not.

Code: `globe/engine/moon.ts` (the maths and the sprite, pure, `moon.test.ts`), `globe/engine/moon-overlay.ts` (the DOM: one `<a class="globe-moon">`), `GlobeRenderer.moonFrame()` (what the globe tells it), the "moon" block of `app.css`; the app hands the words in as `GlobeProps.moon` (`routes/shell.tsx`). Check: `scripts/home/moon.mjs`.

- **A real link, not a thing in the scene.** The keyboard reaches it (a tab stop, the card shows with `:focus-visible`, Escape closes it), a screen reader reads it (`aria-label` = title, description and call to action), middle click and "open in a new tab" work, and the pointer needs no picking code. It lives in `.globe-moon-root` over the labels, under the page's own controls.
- **On the screen's ellipse, not in 3D.** An ellipse about the planet's centre sized from the silhouette's radius (`MOON`: 1.34 x 0.36 radii, leant 14 degrees, its centre 0.42 radii above the planet's so the near half passes over the north, clear of the caption's rows, and the far half goes behind). The near half is 20 % larger and in front; the planet hides the far half and gives it back softly over the moon's own radius at the limb. A narrow window shrinks the ellipse to fit (never nearer the limb than 1.12 radii). It is on the art grid (a whole cell) like everything the globe draws, and its sprite is 9 to 27 cells: an outline, a body in the page colour (the dark scheme's moon is the light thing), a shaded side dithered by the 4x4 Bayer matrix, five fixed craters (the moon shows one face).
- **When it shows.** At the world view only: `moonAmount` takes it away between 0.25 and 0.9 zoom levels above the fit, so it is gone before the first places are named; also not while a place is open (the inset), the street map or the interlude has the view, or the context is lost. Where the hero's text blocks are (`CoverBlock`) it fades out over 18 px, so it never passes under the name or the rows. Away from the planet's hidden half and the hero's text it is not a target and not a tab stop.
- **Energy.** One lap is 90 s, drawn ten times a second by its own timer (a transform and, on a size change, a few dozen pixels: no frame of the globe), and only while the globe may move: the energy pass's holds (idle for 30 s, a blurred window, off screen, data saver), a hidden tab and reduced motion all leave it standing where it is, still a link (reduced motion: at its rest place, in front and to the right). It holds still while the card is out (so it can be clicked).
- **Touch.** No hover: the first tap shows the card, the card's own link is the second.
- **Not used:** the credits dialog (map credits) and the header (full).

## Header

Restyled with the hero (the board's header): `Globe` (the home page; accessible name "Globe, Elliot Négrel-Jerzy home"), Software, Articles, Artworks, Poetry in mono capitals (`.nav-link`: 12 px, tracking 0.14em and 44 px between items from a 44 rem wide row, shrinking by container width down to 11 px / 6 px on a 320 px phone), the current one in the foreground and underlined, the rest muted, the pointer or keyboard turning an item to `--signal-text`. The search trigger is one control at every width (`data-slot="search-trigger"`, 44 px target, as tall as the links), in the same type: the search icon, "Search" and, from 36 rem of row, the key hint; the icon and the command key are the same size (1.1 em of the 12 px type, 13 px; the hint's own 13 px is not used here); a row under 22 rem (a 320 px phone) keeps the word alone. The hero's own search line keeps its 20 px icon at the head of the line, far from its key hint at the other end (`hero.mjs` measures both).

**On the home page there is no header at all while the hero is at rest** (owner, 2026-10-13, twice: first "no section tabs, the hero's rows are the sections", then "no top bar element at all: the hero's own search line has the search and the key hint"). `Navbar` renders no `<header>`: no bar, no tabs, no search trigger, no fade. What keeps the page accessible: the skip links are unchanged; the first controls in the tab order are the hero's search line and its four rows; the hero's list is a `nav` with `aria-label="Primary"` (the only Primary navigation in the accessibility tree); Cmd/Ctrl-K opens the palette as everywhere (the palette is mounted by `Navbar` beside the header, not inside it, so it also stays open when the header comes or goes). **The header returns whenever the hero is not at rest** (tabs and search, with the plain key hint): collapsed after a gesture, or zoomed in (the rows are `visibility: hidden` then, and zoomed in the hero never returns on its own, so without this the visitor would have no way to the lists but the search); it goes again when the hero returns. Mechanism: `LandingHero` publishes `state === "rest"` to `lib/hero-rest.ts` (a tiny external store) and `Navbar` reads it, because the header is a sibling of the shell in the root layout. The server and the first client render say "at rest", like the hero's server markup, so nothing differs at hydration. Every other route, including a place or an entry opened from the globe, has the header unchanged. **The header that a gesture brings ignores the pointer for a moment** (2026-10-13, found in round 3 of the hero, present since the header came back on collapse): on a phone a tap on the globe collapses the hero in the same task as the press, the header row is under the finger when it lifts, and the tap's click used to activate the link there (a tap at the very top navigated to "Articles"). `Navbar`'s `useGestureGuard` sets `data-guard` on a header that appears because the hero left rest on `/`, and `app.css` makes its controls `pointer-events: none` until the pointer sequence in progress has ended plus `GESTURE_GUARD_MS` (350 ms), and in any case `GESTURE_GUARD_CAP_MS` (1.5 s) after the header appeared (`lib/gesture-guard.ts`: pointers are tracked by id and forgotten on blur or a hidden tab, so a pointerup that never came cannot leave the header inert); the click lands on the globe, which has none. The header stays visible; keyboard focus and assistive technology are untouched (they do not use the pointer). `hero.mjs` section 11 taps where the link will appear (390 x 844, touch) and requires the page to stay on `/`, then a real tap on the link after the guard to navigate.
