# The activity picture (software page)

The 3D picture on `/software`: a year of GitHub contributions (green, on top) and WakaTime coding time (blue, below) as glass blocks, one column a day. Code: `apps/web/app/components/activity-panel.tsx` (the panel, figures, legend), `activity-scene.tsx` (the scene, raw three.js), `ground-light.ts` (the light on the ground), `neon.ts` (the start-up), `lib/activity.ts` and `lib/activity-view.ts` (the data and the geometry shared with the place-holder), `lib/glass-caustics.ts` (the readable reference of the optics, with the tests). The data is `activity.json` on the `data` branch (`.github/workflows/activity.yml`, every 6 hours, `[vercel skip]`).

## How the light is made

- **Light theme**: the sun through the glass, traced on the graphics card (`ground-light.ts`, a vertex shader; the same optics as `lib/glass-caustics.ts`): refraction, Fresnel, total internal reflection, the glass's colour absorption, rounded edges. The ground's brightness is a signed difference to what it would have got with no glass, so shadows are coloured and soft and caustics are the surplus.
- **Dark theme**: there is no sun, the light is each block's rod. What one block casts on the ground is **baked**: `app/assets/caustic-templates.png` (41 KB) holds path-traced templates (Mitsuba 3) of a glass block with a rod in it, for 3 heights x 4 heights above the ground, averaged over the square's symmetries so they are clean. The page lays the right template under every block (`stamps` in `ground-light.ts`), in the rod's colour. Tracing this live was tried and does not work: with the rays the page can afford the result is noise.
- Regenerating the templates (only if the blocks' shape or the rod changes): `scripts/caustic-templates/README.md`.

### Tunables (all in `activity-scene.tsx` unless said)

`CAUSTIC_POWER` (dark light strength), `STAMP_CONTRAST` and `STAMP_FOOT` (`ground-light.ts`: how much of the faint wide glow and of the bright foot of a block is kept), `POOL` (the soft haze), `ABSORB`, `STRAIGHT`, `SHADOW`, `GLEAM` (light theme), the bloom's blue/green factors in the show shader, `OFF_EMISSIVE`, `OFF_ENV` and the rod's off colour (the neon's off state).

## The neon start-up (dark theme)

When the picture first appears in the dark theme the rods flicker on like gas tubes: the blocks are split into 8 oval areas (not square), each with its own stutter, same for all its blocks (a few ms and a little brightness off from one another), then a warm-up. A block whose rod is off is dim and the same for all (cool grey), lit by the blocks around it that are on. The ground light follows each rod. Sound is synthesised (Web Audio, no file): a low 100 Hz ballast hum that dies away slowly and short bell-like dings (a third of them low), faint; it plays only if the browser lets the page (not before the visitor has interacted with the page). Once everything is on, at first every 3-20 s, then less and less (hardly ever after a few minutes), a batch of tubes (often a whole segment of up to 18 next to one another, sometimes a few, rarely one) flickers like a sign's: out with a lower ding, sometimes a stutter, back on with a higher one; completely random, only while the picture is in view and the tab visible, nothing changes in the final picture. It is skipped for `prefers-reduced-motion`. It runs on every landing on the dark theme; there is no flag (it is skipped for `prefers-reduced-motion`).

Dev-only helpers (not in the production build): `?lite` (the light version on any machine), `?neonhold` (the picture held half on, half off; it needs the dev server), and the `data-*` figures on the picture's box (`data-frame-ms`, `data-neon-ticks`, `data-neon-apply-ms`, `data-neon-frame-ms`).

## Testing checklist (the neon plays for everyone; run it on the PR preview or the production build)

Measured so far (dev build, Apple M4): an update of the neon costs about 0.05 ms, a frame about 0.5 ms. Emulated Pixel 7 (`activity-neon.mjs`, CPU x6, the Mac's GPU): update 0.7-0.8 ms, frame 4-5 ms, rAF p95 16.8 ms during the start-up; the page's one-time build stalls the main thread for up to 1.3 s at x6 (dev build, cold). None of this is a real phone's GPU.

1. **Emulated, repeatable**: `pnpm --filter @catalyst/web dev`, then `node apps/web/scripts/perf/activity-neon.mjs --device pixel7 --throttle 6` (also `--device mobile`, `--throttle 4` and `--throttle 10`). Look at `rafMs.p95`, `longFrames` and `dataset.neonFrameMs`. Repeat on a **production build** (the dev figures above include Vite's compile; a production build has no `data-*`, so use the rAF figures).
2. **Real devices** (a production build, on the real network: the PR's Vercel preview will do): a recent iPhone (Safari), a mid-range and a low-end Android (Chrome), an older laptop with integrated graphics, a 4K or 5K desktop display. For each: does the start-up run smoothly (no visible stutter beyond the flicker), does the page stay scrollable during it, does the picture end in the same state as with the start-up skipped (reduced motion), does the device warm up or the battery drop noticeably over 5 loads in a row.
3. **Sound**: with sound on and off, silent after a direct reload (blocked by the browser, nothing else breaks), audible after the visitor has clicked; not startling at the system's usual volume; nothing plays when the tab is in the background or after the visitor leaves the page; not played under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
4. **States**: switch light/dark theme during the start-up (it restarts cleanly, no stuck dim blocks); resize and rotate the phone during it; scroll the picture off and back; reduced motion; a page with the data failing to load (no picture, no error).
5. **Both themes, all widths**: the legend and figures at 1300, 1700 and 2560 px; the phone layout (4 strips, bleeding off the edges); the dark glow's brightness against the cubes' (the ground must never be brighter than the blocks).
6. **Open decisions**: the sound has no visible switch (it plays when the browser allows it and is silent otherwise; add a mute control if that is not enough); cap the update rate on `lite` further if a real phone needs it (`fps` in `createNeon`, and `samplesCap` in `activity-scene.tsx`), and rerun 1-5 on the production build.

## Layout (software page)

- From 48 rem (768 px) the picture is one long strip and the page's and the year's figures are in ONE grid in its top right corner (`Figures` in `activity-panel.tsx`; the head's own figures are hidden then). From 56 rem the legend (the GitHub / WakaTime links) is in the bottom left corner too; below that it is under the picture. The grid and the legend scale with the page (`cqi`).
- Below 48 rem (a phone) the picture is four strips, staggered along x by the amount that cancels the camera's tilt (`slabFor` in `lib/activity-view.ts`: the strips are stacked straight on the screen, and the picture is about a third narrower than with the strips side by side), in a box of aspect 0.8; the figures are in the head and under the picture.
- Hovering a day (dark theme): after 70 ms its blocks glow (x1.7) and the others dim (x0.42), eased; `FOCUS_ON` / `FOCUS_OFF` in `activity-scene.tsx`. The light theme has none yet.
- Easter egg demo: `/software?xmas` lights every rod in a Christmas-tree colour (red, green, gold, blue, and warm whites, a day always the same one), with the glass left clear and the baked ground light in the rod's colour (dark theme; `xmas` in `activity-scene.tsx`). Dark theme only (the light theme shows the normal picture); a white "Merry Christmas" in the globe's calligraphy floats over the picture's bottom right and fades on hover (its baseline is measured onto the legend's last line). Every 5-11 s a faint shake of synthesised sleigh bells (`components/sleigh-bells.ts`, same gating as the neon's idle sounds: only once the browser lets the page play, in view, tab visible). Not on by default.
