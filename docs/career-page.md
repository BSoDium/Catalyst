# Career page (`/career`)

The curriculum vitae as a page, for the people who read it (and print it): `apps/web/app/routes/career.tsx`, `components/career-page.tsx`, `lib/career.ts` (dates, languages, structured data: pure), `lib/career-meta.ts` (head tags), styles at the end of `app/app.css` ("The career page"). Data: the optional top-level `career` of the projection ([api-contract.md](api-contract.md#the-career-document-career)); there is no other source and no LLM.

## When it exists

Only while the published projection has a `career` (the owner's private document has `status: published`), or in `pnpm dev` (the preview carries the draft) and on demo content. Without one: `/career` is a real 404 (the site's not-found page), there is no link to it anywhere (header, hero, search), it is not in `sitemap.xml`, and nothing else changes: production has none until the owner publishes. The root loader's `hasCareer` feeds the header and the hero (`lib/use-has-career.ts`); the search index has the row only when the career exists; `scripts/prod/check.mjs` checks the right case from the sitemap (404 and no link, or the page's head, h1, JSON-LD and no contact channel).

## Where it is reached from (owner decisions, 2026-10-14)

- **Home hero:** a fifth quiet row "Career" after Poetry (same row style, arrow, no count; phone too). It is not a content kind, so the caption's section names are unchanged ([landing.md](landing.md)).
- **Header:** a "Career" link after the four sections and before the search. Seven items need tighter tiers (`[data-career]` in app.css); under 24 rem of header (a phone narrower than 384 px) the link is left out there: the hero row and the search have it.
- **Search palette:** one row, type `career` (words: career, CV, resume, experience, education, employers and skills named in it).
- Not a section: no list, no count, not in the sections table (`lib/sections.ts`).

## Layout

(Under 56 rem the running head is not drawn: the header's selector already says "Career", and "Updated Month YYYY" moves under the availability line, `.career-updated`.) Running head over one hairline (the document's title at the left, "Updated Month YYYY" at the right), the owner's name as the h1 at the list-title scale with the optional portrait beside it (a SQUARE from 40 rem, its top level with the name's and its height the text block's, down to the block's bottom, up to a reserved column `--portrait-col` = `clamp(11rem, 34cqi, 22rem)`: the column is padding on the text and the picture is out of the flow, so the text's width never depends on the picture (a square sized from the text's height IN the flow made it blow up at about 820 px), `object-fit: cover`, `object-position: 50% 30%`, no frame of the page's (the picture draws its own), no radius; below 40 rem it is the BACKDROP of the head instead of shrinking beside a tall column of text: the picture spans the head's width as a square, under the running head's rule, and the name and the statement are drawn over its lower part (from about two thirds down, 36 % of the width of overlap) on a gradient from the page colour (`.career-portrait::after`, an eased fall like the nav's scrim: solid behind the statement, 82 % or more behind the name); the DOM keeps the name first, `order: -1` puts the picture first; an SVG with its own `prefers-color-scheme` rule follows the scheme, see `docs/portrait/portrait.svg`, width and height attributes, `loading="eager"`, `fetchpriority="high"`; none = the name block takes the room, no frame), the profile statement, the optional availability line ("... · as of September 2026": the month only, never a relative time), then:

1. **Languages: one compact line directly under the head.** The owner asked whether languages should be higher; they are: a recruiter's first filter is "does this person work in my language", the line costs one row, and a footer would be below the fold on a phone. They keep the owner's other idea (skills after education) intact.
2. **Experience**: an ordered list, newest first as authored, two columns from 64 rem (who, when and where at the left in a fixed 26 rem; highlights and skills at the right), one faint 1 px rail with a 41 px marker per item (the organisation's logo, else the role's icon) (`careerIcon` picks the icon from the title: rocket for a founder, layers for full-stack, code for front-end, palette for design, briefcase otherwise; education: cuboid, sigma, cap; the role in progress is accent-coloured), no boxes; each item: organisation (h3, linked to `orgUrl` when there is one) with the location at the right (linked to the place's page when the place exists), role title, one muted line `employment | period (`<time>`) | length of the stay`, highlights, role skills as quiet chips.
3. **Education**: the same timeline.
4. **Skills**: groups as a definition list, items comma separated (compact).
5. **Socials**: the document's public links (GitHub, ...), https only, `rel="me noopener noreferrer"`. No address, phone number or mailto link anywhere (the contract has none; a Copy-email button is a later task): the way to reach the owner is the contact form just above (below).

## Contact

(Owner, 2026-10-30: "a footer in the career page, with a button at the top, in the header with my name, linking to it".) The contact form closes the page, as the RIGHT half of a pair with the socials at the left (owner, 2026-10-30; the way education and skills are, from 80 rem; stacked below it, the form first: `order` in CSS, the markup keeps the form first): `components/contact-form.tsx`, `lib/contact.ts`, `lib/use-contact-context.ts`, id `contact`. A quiet secondary button "Get in touch" under the availability line in the head links to it (`#contact`: the section takes the focus, `scroll-margin-top` clears the header). Name, email, message; one button.

- **Transport:** [Web3Forms](https://web3forms.com), a hosted form-to-email relay. The key (`CONTACT_ACCESS_KEY`) is public by design (it names the inbox, nothing else); what limits it is the allowed-domains list and the rate limit in the owner's Web3Forms dashboard, to be set to the production host. No backend, no mail provider secret, and the owner's address is never in the repo, the HTML or the projection.
- **Without the script it is a plain HTML form** (owner, 2026-10-30): `action` the relay, `method="post"`, hidden `access_key`, `subject` and `redirect` (the career page with `?sent=1#contact`, built from the root loader's `siteUrl`: a free Web3Forms plan redirects to the key's own domain only), `required`, `type="email"` and `minLength` for the browser's own validation, a honeypot. The CSP's `form-action` names the relay (`CONTACT_ORIGIN`), and `connect-src` does too. Web3Forms sends the visitor back to `/career?sent=1`, where the form says thanks. With the script, `noValidate` turns on and the post stays on the page (`fetch`, JSON).
- **Behaviour (script):** validated on submit (`validateContact`: a name, one address, 10 to 4000 characters), each error is text under its field (and `aria-invalid`), the first wrong field takes the focus; sending disables the button and says "Sending…" (a second submit is ignored); a sent form is not replaced (that moved the page): its thanks, with a check, is laid over it in the same grid cell (`.contact-stage`: the form fades out and goes inert, the height is kept), a polite live region that takes the focus, and after 2.6 s the block eases down to the thanks' height (no motion under reduced motion); sending keeps the button's own colours (`aria-busy`, not `disabled`, which drew it grey); a refusal or a network failure says so in words, re-enables the button and keeps what was typed. A honeypot field (`botcheck`, off-screen, out of the tab order) answers "sent" without calling anything.
- **Look:** bare fields as the rest of the site (owner, 2026-10-30: rounded corners were "out of place" and were reverted): a 1 px box in the page's hairline (a step deeper under the pointer: owner, 2026-10-30, "more subtle"), no fill, no radius, the reading face for what is typed, the ink's focus ring. The head's name sets the family name a step lighter (300) and dimmer (a solid mix of the ink and the page) than the given name.
- **Not part of print.** The search finds the career row by "contact", "email", "hire" and "message" too (no row of its own). **Not on the home page** for now (the hero's rows lead to content; Career is one step away and the search finds it): to be revisited with real traffic.
- **Checked:** unit tests (`lib/contact.test.ts`, the page's markup, the CSP); in the browser with the network stubbed: empty and wrong fields, the loading state, a refusal, a network failure (text kept, button back), a success, the server-rendered form and the `?sent=1` return. **Not verified:** a real delivery, the real redirect (it needs the production domain registered with the key), the plain post in a browser without the script, Safari and iOS, a screen reader.

Headings: h1 name; h2 Languages (visually a label), Experience, Education, Skills, Socials; h3 organisation or institution. Periods: `Sep 2025 – Present`, a missing end shows the start alone, a year-only date stays a year. Solid colours of the page tokens only (no opacity), no animation at all (so reduced motion has nothing to switch off), no layout shift (sizes known).

## Head tags and structured data

Title `Career · <name>`, description the profile statement cut at 160 characters, canonical, the site's default `og:image` (the portrait is never the share image and never in the structured data), one JSON-LD `ProfilePage` about a `Person` with only the site's own name and role line, `url` and `sameAs` (the document's public links); no employer, place, image or contact detail. Sitemap: `/career` with `lastmod` = the reviewed month. Robots: as every page (allowed on the production host, disallowed elsewhere).

## Print

Chrome (header, skip links, scrim) hidden; ink on white whatever the scheme; one column (the blocks stack), items never split, headings keep with their content, the portrait at 3 cm, links print their address after the text, 10.5 pt text. A4 PDF of the demo: 2 pages.

## Portrait

Contract: `career.portrait` `{ src, alt, width, height, dark? }`, the first four required when present, and an optional `logo` `{ src, width, height }` on a role and on an education ([api-contract.md](api-contract.md)): the logo is the item's marker (a file of the page's own, `public/career-logos/`, is the fallback by name), `portrait.dark` the picture for the dark colour scheme. The stay length of a role in progress runs to `career.updated`, never to the clock. Private repo: `portrait: { file: ./portrait.webp, alt, rights: own }` in `content/career/index.md`, the file next to it; exported as `/media/career/portrait.<hash>.<ext>` with its real size, copied by the publication PR; EXIF/XMP, size, rights and alt are enforced by the private validation. The demo has an abstract SVG one.
