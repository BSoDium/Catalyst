earth-grey.png: a grey, 4096 x 2048, 12-step, lightly smoothed reduction (the coast pushed out over the sea) of NASA's Blue Marble "Shaded Relief and Bathymetry" (GIBS layer BlueMarble_ShadedRelief_Bathymetry, level 4 of the Web Mercator tiles, https://gibs.earthdata.nasa.gov), sea removed (0), its brightness matched to the previous 1024 x 512 picture (from NASA Visible Earth's land_shallow_topo_2048.jpg, https://visibleearth.nasa.gov/images/57752), which is also the poles' (past the Mercator range). NASA imagery is in the public domain (https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/). Baked by scripts/globe/bake-earth.py. Used by engine/surface.ts.
